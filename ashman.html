Ash house : A user guide to enhance your stay
Coocking
A century of quiet refinement

Built in 1924 and restored with restraint — original wide-plank floors, board-and-batten walls freshly whitewashed, a stone fireplace that has weathered a hundred winters. Nothing superfluous. Everything considered. The wraparound porch faces the harbor; the back deck catches afternoon sun until dusk.

Laundry
A kitchen that invites staying in

There's a clam shack around the corner worth the trip once. The rest of the week, this kitchen earns its keep — a Wolf six-burner, a lobster pot with two decades of use behind it, sharp knives, and enough counter space to work. Seats twelve inside.
